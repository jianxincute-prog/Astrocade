YOUR WAY OUT

The exit door needs THREE KEYS. None of them lie around: each one is locked behind a puzzle. I — wake the three seals in the right order (read the note on the stand near where you woke). II — open the safe: three torn pages hide three digits. III — repeat the pattern on the lamp console.

THE MICROPHONE

It is not only your feet. If your real voice is LOUD, or you clap or shout, the tall one hears it in here. It is not very sensitive: whispering and normal breathing are fine. Watch the MIC bar — the red line is where it starts to listen.

A NOISE THAT ISN'T YOU

Small things are left everywhere — mugs, staplers, tape. Take one in your hand and throw it. Wherever it lands, that is where the tall one goes. Buy yourself a hallway.

THE TORCH

You carry a lamp. Kill it with L (or the LIGHT button) when you want the dark. It is old: every so often it stutters and dies for a breath, then comes back. That is the lamp, not the dark. Your hand only holds one thing — take an object and the lamp goes in your pocket.

IF IT FINDS YOU

Break its sight behind a wall. Crouch. Do not move. It follows the last sound it understands, not the silence after it.
