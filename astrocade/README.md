# Astro World

WorldBox-style god-sim sandbox. Portrait 720x1280, canvas + DOM overlay, no external assets.

- `game.js` — everything: procedural world, creature AI, fire/disasters, rendering, input, UI, edit/play modes. Creature and house sprites are pixel art defined in code (walk A/B, attack C): sizes vary per species (rabbit 11x10 up to the 14x16 werewolf), lit from the upper left.
- `game_config.json` — `settings` (day/night, starting population scale) and `world` (`size` 0/1/2 = S/M/L, seed, RLE tiles painted in edit mode).

Play mode: full sim + tools + autosave via `lib.saveUserGameState`.
Edit mode: sim frozen; sculpt terrain / regenerate world; changes are written to `gameConfig.world`.
Zoom: + / − buttons only; minimum zoom is the full-map view.

World sizes: S 120x148, M 200x246 (default), L 240x296. M/L use a multi-continent generator; population caps scale with size.
Far zoom (tile < 5px) draws dots instead of sprites. Destructive world actions need a second tap.

Houses: one architectural style per race (human straw cottage, elf treehouse, dwarf stone hall, orc hide tent, werewolf moon lodge) with chimney smoke, lit windows at night and forge/moon glow.

Kingdoms (phase 1 of civilization): houses of one race within 9 tiles form a kingdom with a generated name, a capital and a territory (tinted area with a border line; toggle with the flag button in the world tab). Each kingdom has food/wood/stone/gold: food limits births and famine kills, wood pays for new houses, and kingdoms level up Tribe -> Village -> Town -> Kingdom when population, houses and resources allow. Villages get a capital banner, Towns/Kingdoms a bigger per-race capital hall, Kingdoms a royal aura; the oldest member is crowned. Tap a house or territory with the inspect tool for a summary. Save format v5 stores kingdoms and each house's kingdom.

Technology (phase 2): each race has 4 sequential techs researched automatically once the kingdom has the level and resources (30 s of research): human farming/walls/knighthood/trade, elf living forest/archery/fire ward/tree of life, dwarf mining/forge/vault/mountain hold, orc war camp/raiding/blood totem/horde, werewolf moon prayer/pack/spirit totem/full moon. Effects are multipliers and flags on the kingdom (`k.mod`): production, births, attack/defence, range (elf arrows), fire immunity, loot, lifesteal, night strength, regeneration, bigger territory/house cap. Visible results: farm fields, stone wall ring, mine, war banner, blood totem, spirit totem and tree of life landmarks, floating tech icons. The 📖 Knowledge power (👑 tab) grants the next tech instantly. Save format v6 stores how many techs each kingdom has (v5 still loads).

War & diplomacy (phase 3, fully automatic): every ~10 s kingdoms on the same landmass and within reach evaluate each other. Aggression per race (orc high, human/dwarf low, elf very low, **werewolves never declare war**); a kingdom starts at most one war itself, needs enough population (and level 1 unless orc), and picks fights more readily against weaker or overlapping neighbours. Friendly races (human/elf/dwarf) may sign alliances and allies join defensive wars; wars end when a side is beaten (<=1 house or <=2 people) or from war weariness, followed by a 4000-tick truce. Soldiers (25-55% of a kingdom, plus its leader) march on the enemy capital and fight kingdom enemies instead of race enemies; non-orc races capture adjacent enemy houses, orcs raze and loot them. A werewolf kingdom stays neutral until attacked: once at war its soldiers fight back, and peace returns it to neutrality. Visible: red war-cry rings, crossed swords over capitals at war, dashed red lines between warring capitals (with borders on), peace/war toasts, and enemies/allies in the kingdom panel. Save format v7 stores relations.

Events & realm powers (phase 4): kingdoms suffer or enjoy random events: ☠️ plague (kills people, cuts births, spreads to touching neighbours, then 4000 ticks of immunity), 🥀 drought (food x0.4) and ✨ golden age (births and food up). Unrest builds from famine, plague and long wars and fades in peace; when it maxes out in a mature kingdom (level 2+, 8+ houses, 6000-tick cooldown) one side of the realm **rebels** as a new kingdom already at war with its parent. Tiny kingdoms (<=3 houses) at peace are annexed by a bigger neighbour of their race, which keeps fragmentation in check. The 👑 tab gives the player 🙏 Grace (resources, cures plague, golden age, calms unrest), ☠️ Plague, 🔥 Rebellion (incite a rebellion), 🕊️ Peace (end all wars of the kingdoms touched), 🤝 Unite (merge with the nearest kingdom of the same race), ✂️ Split (peaceful split) and 📖 Knowledge; they act once per touch. Events show as an icon and aura over the capital, the panel lists the event and unrest. Save format v8 stores events and unrest.

Leaderboard: the 🏆 button (world tab, play mode) submits the **civilization score** = sum over kingdoms of 2 x population + 3 x houses + 60 x level + 40 x techs, plus 2 per year survived. Only a new personal best is submitted (otherwise the top 5 is shown); a quiet auto-submit runs every minute when the score grew by 10%. The best sent score is stored in the save (`bs`). Higher is better.

Wildlife: sheep, rabbits and deer are prey (rabbits/deer flee); wolves hunt them, avoid settlements when fed, and a small pack migrates in from wild land when wolves get scarce so they never stay extinct.

Forced evolution: when only 2-3 wolves remain they all evolve into werewolves (cooldown 1200 ticks). Werewolves are a neutral civilization: nobody is their foe, they fight nobody, and they grow slowly (rare births, few houses scaled to population, long lives, small cap).

HUD & icons: no emoji are rendered. Strings in game.js still use emoji as tokens, but `EMOJI_ICON` maps each to a hand-drawn 12x12 outlined pixel icon (`ICON_ART`; creature portraits are cut from the sprite atlas) and a MutationObserver swaps them for `<img>` in the DOM; the world canvas draws the same icons with `drawIcon`. The top bar is 190px: buttons + year, then two chip rows (people/kingdoms/houses, wildlife/wars/plague). Buttons are bevelled with a gold active state.

Natural disasters (automatic, play mode): every ~10 s a check may schedule lightning (minor; can start forest fires), a volcano eruption on a mountain (smoke warning ~6 s, then lava vent + streams + fires) or a meteor (warning ~4 s). Volcano/meteor never target spots near kingdoms of <=3 houses and have a cooldown (1500 ticks on Jarang, 600 on Sering). The ☄️ button in the world tab cycles Dead / Jarang (default) / Sering; the level is saved as `dl` in the v8 save. Warnings show a dashed red ring with an icon and a toast.

Disasters, stage 2: earthquake (🪨, hits a kingdom with 6+ houses: ~25% of houses within 9 tiles collapse, screen shakes, a few creatures are hurt) and flood/tsunami (🌊, the sea surges up to 6 tiles inland from a sandy coast, destroying houses and drowning creatures on the flooded tiles; it recedes after ~80 s and flooded land is saved dry). They share the volcano/meteor cooldown; roll weights are volcano 40%, meteor 20%, quake 25%, flood 15%.

Town props (cosmetic, derived from kingdom state each draw, nothing extra saved): every race has its own wall, corner tower, gate, well, field and granary art (`PROPS`): human stone wall/red-roof tower/timber barn/wheat, elf living hedge wall/tree tower/flower beds, dwarf iron-banded stone/forge tower/portcullis/mushroom beds, orc spiked palisade/skull-banner watchtower/hide hut/meat racks, werewolf bone-and-pelt fence/antler totem tower/moon-glow pool/night herbs. A wall ring with gates on all four sides surrounds the capital from level 2 (or with the human Walls tech), larger with corner towers at level 3. Wells appear from 4 houses, granaries from level 1, fields from 3 houses (more per house, plus extra with farming techs). Props never draw on top of houses.

Walls are real barriers in war: the wall ring (level 2+, or the human Walls tech) blocks every creature of a kingdom that is at war with the wall's owner (soldiers and civilians alike). Gate tiles in the middle of each side stay open, so attackers that bump into a wall detour to the nearest gate (`moveToward`, `wallBlocks`, barrier maps `wallK`/`wallG` rebuilt every 16 ticks). In peace, for allies, neutral werewolves and wildlife, walls do not block. The ring is anchored to a stable centre (`ringCenter`) so it does not jump when the capital changes. Elf archers still shoot over walls.

Siege: a soldier blocked by an enemy wall may hit it instead of walking to a gate (chance per blocked move: orc 70%, dwarf 40%, werewolf 30%, human 15%, elf 8%; damage = race damage x siege multiplier orc 2.5 / dwarf 1.6 / werewolf 1.2 / human 1 / elf 0.7, x attacker atk, / owner def). Wall segments take 60 damage, towers 120; damage shows as darkening and cracks. A broken segment leaves rubble and stops blocking. Kingdoms at peace repair damage (-25 per 10 s) and rebuild breached segments for 3 wood each. `warStats.breached` counts breaches. Gates are never destroyed (they are open anyway).

Defence: intact corner towers (level 2+ rings) shoot the nearest enemy soldier in range every 1.5 s during war (damage 4+level, scaled by atk, divided by the target's def; range 6, 7 at level 3, +2 for elf archery); a broken tower stops firing. About one in three non-soldier civilians of a besieged kingdom walks to the nearest damaged wall segment and mends it (22 damage per action for 0.5 wood; a breached segment costs 3 wood to rebuild). `warStats.repaired` / `towerShots` count both.

Siege engines: a kingdom at war with a walled enemy (and 12+ people, enough wood/stone, 20 s between builds, max 2-3 alive) builds an engine at its capital: orcs a battering ram (25 wood; 90 hp, hits a wall every ~1.5 s for 8 x4 damage and works its way around the ring), dwarves an iron catapult and human level 2+ a wooden catapult (20 wood + 15 stone; 60-70 hp, lobs rocks at walls/towers from up to 7 tiles, and bombards enemy soldiers with splash damage once the walls are down). Engines are real creatures (`engine:1` in `RACES`, sprites `ram`/`catapult`/`dcatapult`), so defenders and towers can kill them, but they are not saved, never count as population or leaders, and are dismantled when the war ends or after ~5 min.

Resources & trees: the world now holds ore nodes — stone, bronze (copper), iron, silver and gold (`oreT`/`oreA`, sprites in `ORE_SPR`) — seeded on hills and mountains (stone also on grass) and respawned slowly so the world never runs dry (`resTick`). Each second a kingdom works one random node of each kind within reach (11 tiles + 2 per level + radius techs; not inside another kingdom's territory): stone +3, bronze/iron/silver +1, gold +3 per success, 6-12 successes empty a node. Mining speed scales with population and race (dwarf x2, human 1, orc 1.1, elf/werewolf 0.7); bronze, silver and gold need level 1, iron level 2 (and bronze tools first), dwarves one level earlier. Stocks: bronze, iron, silver (shown in the kingdom panel). Ages: 20 bronze -> Bronze Age (+10% attack/defence, +35% wall strength), 25 iron -> Iron Age (+22%, +70% walls). Town needs 8 bronze, Kingdom 12 iron + 10 silver, and many techs now cost bronze/iron/silver; a metal requirement is waived when none exists within reach, so there are no dead ends. Trees come in kinds laid out as static groves (oak, pine, birch, fruit tree, plus palms on sand) that differ in timber yield and race affinity (elves love fruit/birch, dwarves pine, humans oak) and fruit trees/coconuts feed people. Save adds `rs`/`ra` (node positions/amounts) and four extra fields per kingdom (silver, bronze, iron, age); older saves just get fresh nodes.

Market & caravans: a world market sets a price (in gold) for food, wood, stone, bronze, iron and silver that follows world-wide stock (plentiful = cheap, scarce = dear, smoothed every 10 s). A kingdom of level 1+ with 5+ houses at peace picks the commodity it lacks most (next level / tech / age needs), finds the nearest peaceful kingdom on the same landmass (within 48 tiles) with a surplus and sends a wagon: it pays up front in gold (or in a surplus good of its own), the wagon walks to the seller, buys up to 20 units at the market price (+0.4% per tile) and carries them home. A lost wagon refunds the payment if the goods were not yet bought. Wagons (`cart`, max 2 per kingdom) are temporary creatures like siege engines and are not saved. The 🏦 button in the world tab shows prices with ▲/▼ trend, wagons on the road and completed trades (which also add 3 points each to the civilization score). Prices and the trade counter are saved (`mk`, `mt`).

Iron knights: a kingdom in the Iron Age (level 2+) with 8 iron arms one of its soldiers near the capital (max 2 + 2 per level above 2 + pop/32, never more than 8): +80% hp, +50% attack, +40% defence, drawn with a helmet, crest and shield over the race sprite and counted in the kingdom panel. Indices of knights are saved in `el`.

Touch controls: with any tool, one finger now pans — drag to move the map (with a little momentum), tap to use the tool at that spot, and hold still for ~0.25 s to switch to painting so the tool can be dragged across the map. Two fingers still pan too, the ✋ hand tool pans immediately, and the inspect tool taps to inspect / drags to pan.

Creature panel: in play mode the default tool is now 🔍 Inspect. Tapping a creature (with the inspect tool, or any spawn tool; terrain/power tools still act on tap, hold-drag paints) selects it: a gold ring marks it on the map and a panel opens above the tool bar with a portrait cut from its sprite, a generated personal name (per race / animal), role tag (Citizen, Soldier, Leader, Iron knight, Hunter, Prey, Merchant, War machine), status (Healthy, Wounded, Dying, Hungry, Burning, At war), a one-line description, HP / energy / age bars, kills and kingdom. The panel stays and updates live (4 times a second), 🎯 makes the camera follow the creature (panning cancels it) and ✖ closes it; when the creature dies the panel shows "Dead" for a moment. Tapping a house, territory or ground with the inspect tool still shows kingdom / tile info.

Individuals (persona): every creature gets 0-2 random traits (Brave +15% attack, Sturdy +25% hp, Swift moves faster, Long-lived +30% lifespan, Frail -20% hp, Gifted +25% weapon effect), a stable name seed, and settlers keep a life history (last 10 events: first kill and 5/10/25 victories, crowned leader, iron knight, nearly died, new weapon/armour, joining a kingdom; birth year derived from age). Equipment has three slots — weapon, armour, shield — in three tiers matching the kingdom's age (stone/wood, bronze, iron) with race-specific items (human spear/sword, elf bows, dwarf hammers/axes, orc clubs/cleavers, werewolf claws). Every ~10 s a kingdom arms its leader, iron knights and soldiers (8 items per tick) for wood / bronze / iron: weapons add up to +40% attack, armour up to +35% defence and +9 hp, shields up to +18% defence; gear is drawn on the sprite in tier colours and listed in the creature panel with traits and history. The old flat Bronze/Iron Age attack bonus was replaced by this gear (kingdom age still strengthens walls and unlocks the next gear tier). Iron knights now get x1.25 attack, x1.2 defence and x1.5 hp on top. Save adds `ex` (nid, traits, birth tick, kills, gear, history per settler); animals just re-roll traits and names on load.

Family & bag: settlers born in a house now get two adult parents of their kingdom living nearby (`setFamily`): traits are inherited (50% per trait, 85% if both parents have it, plus a 12% mutation), parents become spouses when unattached, and children, spouse and parents are tracked (`pa`, `pb`, `sp`, `ch`, saved in `ex`; new ids never collide with saved name seeds). The creature panel of a settler has three tabs: Info (description, bars, last 4 life events incl. births, pairings, children, gear given/taken), Family (spouse, parents, children — living relatives are buttons that jump to them, dead ones are marked †) and Bag (universal bag, 12 slots). Equipment slots in the panel are buttons: tap an occupied slot to take the item into the bag, or select a bag item and tap the matching slot to wear it (the old item goes to the bag). Bag items are `[slot, tier, origin race]` and any race can use any item (it takes on the wearer's race look and name); the bag is saved as `inv`. Kingdoms still re-arm soldiers that lack gear, at their own cost.

Ground loot: gear can lie on the ground (`drops`, max 120, rusts away after ~5 min, saved as `dr`). A settler that dies drops each worn item with 70% chance; a victor first takes any better piece from the body it just killed (`lootFrom`, history "Looted ..."). Settlers wear a found item if it beats their own (the old one stays on the ground), idle soldiers/leaders/knights walk to improving gear within 7 tiles (`seekDrop`), and tapping an item with the inspect or a spawn tool puts it into the bag. In the bag tab, a selected item can be dropped two tiles in front of the creature ("Drop") for others to find. Drops are drawn as the slot icon with a tier-coloured glow.

Enchanted items, market sales and royal chests: items are `[slot, tier, origin race, enchant]` and enchantments live in `c.ec[slot]` (note `c.en` is hunters' energy). Weapons: Flaming (20% sets the target ablaze), Venomous (poison 0.35 hp/tick for 40 ticks), Vampiric (25% lifesteal), Storm (15% lightning to a second enemy); armour: Thorned (25% melee reflect), Regenerating (regeneration), Fireproof (fire immunity); shields: Warding (20% block), Guardian (+20% max hp). They come from crafting (4% on tier 2+ gear of level 2+ kingdoms), loot, and royal smiths; enchanted pieces glow in their colour on the sprite, in bag/chest and on the ground. Every kingdom now has a royal chest (6 + 4 per level slots, saved in the kingdom row): non-soldier settlers carry finds from the ground to it, soldiers are armed from it before anything is crafted, replaced pieces go back into it, a smith (level 2+, 15 silver, 800-tick cooldown) enchants one chest item, full chests (or poor kingdoms) sell their least valuable item and richer kingdoms buy interesting ones from the market shop (max 10 items, stale after 3000 ticks). Item value = crafting cost at the market's metal prices x1.6 (x4 enchanted); selling pays 75% of it as gold to the creature's kingdom. The creature panel has a fourth tab "Chest" (the kingdom's chest) and buttons To bag / To chest / Drop / Sell 🪙; the 🏦 board lists the shop and sold/bought counts; a chest icon marks capitals whose chest is not empty.

Visual polish (terrain + effects): terrain pixels are lit from the north-west (slopes facing the light are brighter), rock streaks are stronger on mountains, shallows have animated surf along the coast, sand next to water is darker (wet), lava breathes (bright seams over a dark crust) and glows on its neighbours. At sprite zoom mountain ranges are drawn from hand-made rock and snow-capped peak sprites (3 variants each), hills as grassy mounds, and each world has a few real volcanoes (`volcanoes`, one per ~17000 tiles on big massifs, saved as `vc`): a large cone with a glowing crater, a thin smoke wisp when dormant, and a roaring plume of dark smoke, embers and ash for ~1 minute after an eruption; automatic eruptions now pick these volcanoes (the vent tile itself never turns to lava). Effects use a small particle system (smoke, embers, ash, sparks, debris, dust; `emit`/`drawParts`, max 700): flames with flicker, smoke and embers on burning tiles (houses burn bigger, glow at night), lightning with branches, a ground glow and sparks, meteors that streak in from the sky during the last moments of their warning and land with a flash, fireball, shockwave, debris and a crater of smoke, earthquakes with radiating ground cracks and dust. Disaster warnings now last as long as the real tick countdown (they used to vanish after half a second).

Atmosphere (paket C): the day cycle now tints the world — warm orange dusk, deep-blue night with twinkling stars, rosy dawn (`drawAtmosphere`); soft clouds drift with the wind and throw shadows on the ground (they fade out as you zoom in); weather cycles between clear, rain, storm (darker, lightning flashes and, with disasters on, real bolts) and snow, drawn as screen-space particles; rain and storms put out fires; the chip next to the year shows the current weather icon. Sound (paket D): everything is synthesised with Web Audio (no assets, no network): UI ticks, spawn pops, chimes, fanfare on level-ups, sword hits, arrows, thunder, explosions, earthquake rumble, horns when war is declared, splashes, wolf evolution howl; ambient beds for wind, rain and nearby fires; and a generative score (soft pad chords and plucks with an echo, slower and darker at night, with low drums and a drone while kingdoms are at war). Sounds are only played for events in or near the view. The speaker button in the top bar toggles all audio and is saved (`sn`). Title screen and tour (paket E): the game opens on a cover screen (asset `cover` in `asset_map.json`; falls back to a text title) with Play / Continue, a help button and a sound toggle; the world is paused until you press play, which also unlocks audio. First-time players get a short spotlight tour (tap, drag/hold-to-paint, tool tabs, zoom, pause/speed, sound); it is remembered (`tu`) and can be replayed with the ❓ button on the title screen or in the world tab.

Language: all in-game text is now English (tech, tool, item, trait, enchantment and status names, creature descriptions, panel labels, history lines, toasts, tour and title screen).
