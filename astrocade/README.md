# Astro World

WorldBox-style god-sim sandbox. Portrait 720x1280, canvas + DOM overlay, no external assets.

- `game.js` — everything: procedural world, creature AI, fire/disasters, rendering, input, UI, edit/play modes. Creature and house sprites are pixel art defined in code (walk A/B, attack C): sizes vary per species (rabbit 11x10 up to the 14x16 werewolf), lit from the upper left.
- `game_config.json` — `settings` (day/night, starting population scale) and `world` (`size` 0/1/2 = S/M/L, seed, RLE tiles painted in edit mode).

Play mode: full sim + tools + autosave via `lib.saveUserGameState`.
Edit mode: sim frozen; sculpt terrain / regenerate world; changes are written to `gameConfig.world`.
Zoom: + / − buttons only; minimum zoom is the full-map view.

World sizes: S 120x148, M 200x246 (default), L 240x296. M/L use a multi-continent generator; population caps scale with size.
Far zoom (tile < 5px) draws dots instead of sprites. Destructive world actions need a second tap.

Houses: one architectural style per race (human straw cottage, elf treehouse, dwarf stone hall, orc hide tent, werewolf moon lodge) with chimney smoke, lit windows at night and forge/moon glow.

Kingdoms (phase 1 of civilization): houses of one race within 9 tiles form a kingdom with a generated name, a capital and a territory (tinted area with a border line; toggle with the flag button in the world tab). Each kingdom has food/wood/stone/gold: food limits births and famine kills, wood pays for new houses, and kingdoms level up Tribe -> Village -> Town -> Kingdom when population, houses and resources allow. Villages get a capital banner, Towns/Kingdoms a bigger per-race capital hall, Kingdoms a royal aura; the oldest member is crowned. Tap a house or territory with the inspect tool for a summary. Save format v5 stores kingdoms and each house's kingdom.

Technology (phase 2): each race has 4 sequential techs researched automatically once the kingdom has the level and resources (30 s of research): human farming/walls/knighthood/trade, elf living forest/archery/fire ward/tree of life, dwarf mining/forge/vault/mountain hold, orc war camp/raiding/blood totem/horde, werewolf moon prayer/pack/spirit totem/full moon. Effects are multipliers and flags on the kingdom (`k.mod`): production, births, attack/defence, range (elf arrows), fire immunity, loot, lifesteal, night strength, regeneration, bigger territory/house cap. Visible results: farm fields, stone wall ring, mine, war banner, blood totem, spirit totem and tree of life landmarks, floating tech icons. The 📖 Ilmu power (👑 tab) grants the next tech instantly. Save format v6 stores how many techs each kingdom has (v5 still loads).

War & diplomacy (phase 3, fully automatic): every ~10 s kingdoms on the same landmass and within reach evaluate each other. Aggression per race (orc high, human/dwarf low, elf very low, **werewolves never declare war**); a kingdom starts at most one war itself, needs enough population (and level 1 unless orc), and picks fights more readily against weaker or overlapping neighbours. Friendly races (human/elf/dwarf) may sign alliances and allies join defensive wars; wars end when a side is beaten (<=1 house or <=2 people) or from war weariness, followed by a 4000-tick truce. Soldiers (25-55% of a kingdom, plus its leader) march on the enemy capital and fight kingdom enemies instead of race enemies; non-orc races capture adjacent enemy houses, orcs raze and loot them. A werewolf kingdom stays neutral until attacked: once at war its soldiers fight back, and peace returns it to neutrality. Visible: red war-cry rings, crossed swords over capitals at war, dashed red lines between warring capitals (with borders on), peace/war toasts, and enemies/allies in the kingdom panel. Save format v7 stores relations.

Events & realm powers (phase 4): kingdoms suffer or enjoy random events: ☠️ plague (kills people, cuts births, spreads to touching neighbours, then 4000 ticks of immunity), 🥀 drought (food x0.4) and ✨ golden age (births and food up). Unrest builds from famine, plague and long wars and fades in peace; when it maxes out in a mature kingdom (level 2+, 8+ houses, 6000-tick cooldown) one side of the realm **rebels** as a new kingdom already at war with its parent. Tiny kingdoms (<=3 houses) at peace are annexed by a bigger neighbour of their race, which keeps fragmentation in check. The 👑 tab gives the player 🙏 Anugerah (resources, cures plague, golden age, calms unrest), ☠️ Wabah, 🔥 Pemberontakan (incite a rebellion), 🕊️ Damaikan (end all wars of the kingdoms touched), 🤝 Satukan (merge with the nearest kingdom of the same race), ✂️ Pecah (peaceful split) and 📖 Ilmu; they act once per touch. Events show as an icon and aura over the capital, the panel lists the event and unrest. Save format v8 stores events and unrest.

Leaderboard: the 🏆 button (world tab, play mode) submits the **civilization score** = sum over kingdoms of 2 x population + 3 x houses + 60 x level + 40 x techs, plus 2 per year survived. Only a new personal best is submitted (otherwise the top 5 is shown); a quiet auto-submit runs every minute when the score grew by 10%. The best sent score is stored in the save (`bs`). Higher is better.

Wildlife: sheep, rabbits and deer are prey (rabbits/deer flee); wolves hunt them, avoid settlements when fed, and a small pack migrates in from wild land when wolves get scarce so they never stay extinct.

Forced evolution: when only 2-3 wolves remain they all evolve into werewolves (cooldown 1200 ticks). Werewolves are a neutral civilization: nobody is their foe, they fight nobody, and they grow slowly (rare births, few houses scaled to population, long lives, small cap).

HUD & icons: no emoji are rendered. Strings in game.js still use emoji as tokens, but `EMOJI_ICON` maps each to a hand-drawn 12x12 outlined pixel icon (`ICON_ART`; creature portraits are cut from the sprite atlas) and a MutationObserver swaps them for `<img>` in the DOM; the world canvas draws the same icons with `drawIcon`. The top bar is 190px: buttons + year, then two chip rows (people/kingdoms/houses, wildlife/wars/plague). Buttons are bevelled with a gold active state.

Natural disasters (automatic, play mode): every ~10 s a check may schedule lightning (minor; can start forest fires), a volcano eruption on a mountain (smoke warning ~6 s, then lava vent + streams + fires) or a meteor (warning ~4 s). Volcano/meteor never target spots near kingdoms of <=3 houses and have a cooldown (1500 ticks on Jarang, 600 on Sering). The ☄️ button in the world tab cycles Mati / Jarang (default) / Sering; the level is saved as `dl` in the v8 save. Warnings show a dashed red ring with an icon and a toast.

Disasters, stage 2: earthquake (🪨, hits a kingdom with 6+ houses: ~25% of houses within 9 tiles collapse, screen shakes, a few creatures are hurt) and flood/tsunami (🌊, the sea surges up to 6 tiles inland from a sandy coast, destroying houses and drowning creatures on the flooded tiles; it recedes after ~80 s and flooded land is saved dry). They share the volcano/meteor cooldown; roll weights are volcano 40%, meteor 20%, quake 25%, flood 15%.

Town props (cosmetic, derived from kingdom state each draw, nothing extra saved): every race has its own wall, corner tower, gate, well, field and granary art (`PROPS`): human stone wall/red-roof tower/timber barn/wheat, elf living hedge wall/tree tower/flower beds, dwarf iron-banded stone/forge tower/portcullis/mushroom beds, orc spiked palisade/skull-banner watchtower/hide hut/meat racks, werewolf bone-and-pelt fence/antler totem tower/moon-glow pool/night herbs. A wall ring with gates on all four sides surrounds the capital from level 2 (or with the human Tembok tech), larger with corner towers at level 3. Wells appear from 4 houses, granaries from level 1, fields from 3 houses (more per house, plus extra with farming techs). Props never draw on top of houses.

Walls are real barriers in war: the wall ring (level 2+, or the human Tembok tech) blocks every creature of a kingdom that is at war with the wall's owner (soldiers and civilians alike). Gate tiles in the middle of each side stay open, so attackers that bump into a wall detour to the nearest gate (`moveToward`, `wallBlocks`, barrier maps `wallK`/`wallG` rebuilt every 16 ticks). In peace, for allies, neutral werewolves and wildlife, walls do not block. The ring is anchored to a stable centre (`ringCenter`) so it does not jump when the capital changes. Elf archers still shoot over walls.

Siege: a soldier blocked by an enemy wall may hit it instead of walking to a gate (chance per blocked move: orc 70%, dwarf 40%, werewolf 30%, human 15%, elf 8%; damage = race damage x siege multiplier orc 2.5 / dwarf 1.6 / werewolf 1.2 / human 1 / elf 0.7, x attacker atk, / owner def). Wall segments take 60 damage, towers 120; damage shows as darkening and cracks. A broken segment leaves rubble and stops blocking. Kingdoms at peace repair damage (-25 per 10 s) and rebuild breached segments for 3 wood each. `warStats.breached` counts breaches. Gates are never destroyed (they are open anyway).

Defence: intact corner towers (level 2+ rings) shoot the nearest enemy soldier in range every 1.5 s during war (damage 4+level, scaled by atk, divided by the target's def; range 6, 7 at level 3, +2 for elf archery); a broken tower stops firing. About one in three non-soldier civilians of a besieged kingdom walks to the nearest damaged wall segment and mends it (22 damage per action for 0.5 wood; a breached segment costs 3 wood to rebuild). `warStats.repaired` / `towerShots` count both.

Siege engines: a kingdom at war with a walled enemy (and 12+ people, enough wood/stone, 20 s between builds, max 2-3 alive) builds an engine at its capital: orcs a battering ram (25 wood; 90 hp, hits a wall every ~1.5 s for 8 x4 damage and works its way around the ring), dwarves an iron catapult and human level 2+ a wooden catapult (20 wood + 15 stone; 60-70 hp, lobs rocks at walls/towers from up to 7 tiles, and bombards enemy soldiers with splash damage once the walls are down). Engines are real creatures (`engine:1` in `RACES`, sprites `ram`/`catapult`/`dcatapult`), so defenders and towers can kill them, but they are not saved, never count as population or leaders, and are dismantled when the war ends or after ~5 min.

Resources & trees: the world now holds ore nodes — stone, bronze (copper), iron, silver and gold (`oreT`/`oreA`, sprites in `ORE_SPR`) — seeded on hills and mountains (stone also on grass) and respawned slowly so the world never runs dry (`resTick`). Each second a kingdom works one random node of each kind within reach (11 tiles + 2 per level + radius techs; not inside another kingdom's territory): stone +3, bronze/iron/silver +1, gold +3 per success, 6-12 successes empty a node. Mining speed scales with population and race (dwarf x2, human 1, orc 1.1, elf/werewolf 0.7); bronze, silver and gold need level 1, iron level 2 (and bronze tools first), dwarves one level earlier. Stocks: bronze, iron, silver (shown in the kingdom panel). Ages: 20 bronze -> Bronze Age (+10% attack/defence, +35% wall strength), 25 iron -> Iron Age (+22%, +70% walls). Town needs 8 bronze, Kingdom 12 iron + 10 silver, and many techs now cost bronze/iron/silver; a metal requirement is waived when none exists within reach, so there are no dead ends. Trees come in kinds laid out as static groves (oak, pine, birch, fruit tree, plus palms on sand) that differ in timber yield and race affinity (elves love fruit/birch, dwarves pine, humans oak) and fruit trees/coconuts feed people. Save adds `rs`/`ra` (node positions/amounts) and four extra fields per kingdom (silver, bronze, iron, age); older saves just get fresh nodes.

Market & caravans: a world market sets a price (in gold) for food, wood, stone, bronze, iron and silver that follows world-wide stock (plentiful = cheap, scarce = dear, smoothed every 10 s). A kingdom of level 1+ with 5+ houses at peace picks the commodity it lacks most (next level / tech / age needs), finds the nearest peaceful kingdom on the same landmass (within 48 tiles) with a surplus and sends a wagon: it pays up front in gold (or in a surplus good of its own), the wagon walks to the seller, buys up to 20 units at the market price (+0.4% per tile) and carries them home. A lost wagon refunds the payment if the goods were not yet bought. Wagons (`cart`, max 2 per kingdom) are temporary creatures like siege engines and are not saved. The 🏦 button in the world tab shows prices with ▲/▼ trend, wagons on the road and completed trades (which also add 3 points each to the civilization score). Prices and the trade counter are saved (`mk`, `mt`).

Iron knights: a kingdom in the Iron Age (level 2+) with 8 iron arms one of its soldiers near the capital (max 2 + 2 per level above 2 + pop/32, never more than 8): +80% hp, +50% attack, +40% defence, drawn with a helmet, crest and shield over the race sprite and counted in the kingdom panel. Indices of knights are saved in `el`.
